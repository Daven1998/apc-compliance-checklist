# Algarve Alojamento Local — Glossary of Portuguese Terms

Portuguese compliance terms British villa owners encounter, with plain-English definitions.

---

**AL** — *Alojamento Local*. The Portuguese legal regime for short-term tourist rentals (< 30 days). Governed by Decreto-Lei 76/2024.

**AIMA** — *Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo*. The Portuguese border agency that took over SEF's functions in October 2023. Receives SIBA filings.

**ASAE** — *Autoridade de Segurança Alimentar e Económica*. The Portuguese economic and food safety authority. Inspects AL properties for compliance.

**BUÉ** — *Balcão Único Eletrónico*. The single electronic counter for filing AL registrations and related notifications. Run by AMA at [eportugal.gov.pt](https://eportugal.gov.pt).

**CAE** — *Classificação Portuguesa de Atividades Económicas*. Portuguese activity classification code. AL operations use CAE 55201 (hotels), 55202 (tourism apartments) or 55204 (other short-stay).

**Caderneta Predial** — Property tax record card from Finanças. Required for every AL filing. Available at [portaldasfinancas.gov.pt](https://portaldasfinancas.gov.pt).

**Câmara Municipal** — Town hall / city council. The local authority that approves or opposes AL registrations during the 60-day (90-day in containment areas) opposition window.

**CEAL** — *Contribuição Extraordinária do Alojamento Local*. The 2023 special tax on AL operators. **Scrapped by DL 76/2024** retroactively from 1 January 2024.

**Comunicação Prévia** — Prior notification. The formal notice filed via BUÉ to the câmara declaring intent to operate an AL property.

**CRP** — *Conservatória do Registo Predial*. Property registry. Holds the deed and any liens on the property.

**Decreto-Lei 76/2024** — The October 2024 reform of the AL regime. Restored indefinite licences, scrapped CEAL, gave câmaras control over local AL policy.

**Finanças** — The Portuguese tax authority. Operates [portaldasfinancas.gov.pt](https://portaldasfinancas.gov.pt).

**IES** — *Informação Empresarial Simplificada*. Annual business filing for companies, lodged jointly with Finanças, CRC and Banco de Portugal.

**Início de Atividade** — "Start of activity" declaration filed at Finanças when beginning AL operations. Triggers the CAE code registration.

**IRS** — *Imposto sobre o Rendimento das Pessoas Singulares*. Personal income tax. AL income reported under Categoria F (passive rental) or Categoria B (professional activity).

**IRC** — *Imposto sobre o Rendimento das Pessoas Coletivas*. Corporate income tax. AL income through a Portuguese company taxed at 21% (general) or 17% (small companies on first €25k).

**Lei 23/2007** — Portuguese immigration law. Article 14 mandates SIBA reporting. Article 200 sets the fine schedule.

**Licença de Utilização** — Use licence. The câmara document certifying the property can be legally used as housing. Required for AL filing.

**Livro de Reclamações** — Complaints book. Mandatory for every AL (paper or electronic via livroreclamacoes.pt).

**NIF** — *Número de Identificação Fiscal*. Portuguese tax number. Required for every AL filing. Non-residents apply through Finanças or via fiscal representative.

**Património Predial** — Property assets registered with Finanças.

**RGPD** — *Regulamento Geral sobre a Proteção de Dados*. Portuguese implementation of GDPR. Relevant for AL operators handling guest data.

**RNAL** — *Registo Nacional de Alojamento Local*. The national AL registry at Turismo de Portugal. AL numbers issued here.

**SDEP** — *Single Digital Entry Point*. The EU 2024/1028 portal that aggregates short-term rental platform data and shares it with Member States. Live May 2026.

**SEF** — *Serviço de Estrangeiros e Fronteiras*. The former Portuguese border agency, replaced by AIMA in October 2023. Still referenced in legal texts.

**SIBA** — *Sistema de Informação de Boletins de Alojamento*. Guest reporting system run by AIMA. Filing at [siba.sef.pt](https://siba.sef.pt). Mandatory within 3 working days per non-Portuguese guest.

**Titular** — The titleholder / legal owner of the AL registration. Must match the property owner or hold a lease + delegation.

**Turismo de Portugal** — The national tourism agency. Operates the RNAL registry.

**Zona de Contenção** — Containment area. A geographic zone designated by a câmara under DL 76/2024 where new AL registrations are restricted. Several Algarve areas (Albufeira centre, Lagos centre, Faro centre) are designated.
