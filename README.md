# Algarve AL Compliance Checklist

An open-source checklist for owners and managers of **Alojamento Local (AL)** short-let properties in the Algarve. Built and maintained by [Algarve Property Compliance](https://algarvepropertycompliance.com/) — a UK-based service that handles Portugal's AL paperwork for British and foreign villa owners.

This repo is the public version of the internal pre-submission checklist APC uses when filing a new property on the **Balcão Único Eletrónico** under **Decreto-Lei 76/2024**. It is free to copy, fork, translate and adapt — attribution to the project is appreciated, not required.

---

## Who this is for

- British and foreign owners of villas or apartments in the Algarve currently let on Airbnb / Booking / Vrbo
- Property managers handling third-party AL units
- Estate agents preparing properties for short-let activation
- Accountants advising on AL Category B income

This checklist is **not** a substitute for tax or legal advice. It is an operational list of the recurring documents and obligations that catch most owners off guard. For a managed service, see [algarvepropertycompliance.com](https://algarvepropertycompliance.com/).

## The 9 obligations most foreign owners miss

Each item below links to the relevant Portuguese government source.

### 1. SIBA guest reporting (arrivals **and** departures)
- Every foreign guest must be reported within **3 working days** of arrival and again on departure.
- Operated by **AIMA** (which absorbed SEF in 2024).
- Portal: [siba.ssi.gov.pt](https://siba.ssi.gov.pt)

### 2. AL plaque (apartments only — villas are exempt)
- Apartamentos: mandatory exterior plaque next to entrance, specs set in DL 76/2024 Annex.
- Moradias (detached villas): **exempt** from the plaque rule, but the AL number must appear in all advertising.

### 3. Livro de Reclamações — both physical and electronic
- Physical: bought from Imprensa Nacional Casa da Moeda, kept on-site.
- Electronic: registered at [livroreclamacoes.pt](https://www.livroreclamacoes.pt/).
- Complaints filed electronically must be answered within **15 days**.

### 4. Civil liability insurance — €75,000 minimum
- Mandatory under DL 76/2024.
- Proof must be uploaded to [gov.pt — comprovativo de seguro AL](https://www.gov.pt/servicos/enviar-comprovativo-de-seguro-do-alojamento-local).
- Common policies sit between €50k and €250k; €75k is the floor.

### 5. Tourist tax — only 7 of 16 Algarve municipalities
The seven currently collecting *taxa turística*:
- Loulé
- Albufeira
- Faro
- Portimão
- Lagos
- Vila Real de Santo António
- Olhão

Rate: €1.50–€2.00 per guest per night, **capped at 7 nights**. Returns are filed monthly or quarterly depending on the câmara.

### 6. IRS — Category B, not Category F
AL rental income is **commercial (Category B)**, not passive Category F rental. This affects:
- Allowable deductions
- Social security contributions for the operator
- Whether the simplified or organised accounting regime applies

### 7. VAT — 2025 reform for non-resident owners
From the 2025 VAT amendment, **non-resident AL operators lose the VAT exemption regardless of turnover**. Most UK-resident owners are now in scope from euro 1.

### 8. Fire safety (ANEPC)
- One **ABC powder extinguisher per floor** (min 6kg).
- Smoke detector in every bedroom and circulation area.
- Annual extinguisher service by a certified provider.
- Reference: [prociv.gov.pt — alojamento local](https://prociv.gov.pt/pt/seguranca-contra-incendio/estabelecimentos-especificos/alojamento-local/).

### 9. RNAL data updates — 10-day window
Any change to the operator's data (NIF, address, capacity, modalidade, manager) must be updated in the **Registo Nacional de Alojamento Local** within **10 days** of the change. Late updates can trigger inspection.

## What's not in this checklist (deliberately)

- City-specific bylaws (Loulé, Albufeira and Portimão each have additional rules)
- Containment-area rules (90-day câmara opposition window in certain zones)
- ATA (Autoridade Tributária) registration of NIF for non-resident owners
- EU 2024/1028 short-term rental data-sharing regulation taking effect May 2026

Those sit outside the universal floor — see the [Algarve Property Compliance news section](https://algarvepropertycompliance.com/news/) for ongoing updates.

1. Print or fork.
2. Tick each item against your property.
3. For anything ticked "no" or "unsure", either book a session with a Portuguese lawyer/accountant or use a managed compliance service.
