# Algarve Alojamento Local — Official Resources

Direct links to the Portuguese government portals and legal texts every Algarve AL operator should bookmark.

## Government portals

| Portal | URL | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Balcão Único Eletrónico (BUÉ) | [eportugal.gov.pt](https://eportugal.gov.pt) | File AL comunicação prévia |
| Registo Nacional de Alojamento Local | [rnt.turismodeportugal.pt](https://rnt.turismodeportugal.pt) | Search and verify AL numbers |
| SIBA (guest reporting) | [siba.sef.pt](https://siba.sef.pt) | File guest boletins |
| Portal das Finanças | [portaldasfinancas.gov.pt](https://portaldasfinancas.gov.pt) | NIF, Caderneta, IRS, Início de Atividade |
| Livro de Reclamações | [livroreclamacoes.pt](https://livroreclamacoes.pt) | Electronic complaints book |
| Turismo de Portugal | [turismodeportugal.pt](https://turismodeportugal.pt) | RNAL, modalidades, capacity rules |
| Diário da República | [dre.pt](https://dre.pt) | Official legal texts |

## Legal texts

| Diploma | Subject | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Decreto-Lei 76/2024 | The 2024 AL reform — current governing law | [dre.pt](https://dre.pt) search "76/2024" |
| Lei 23/2007 | Immigration law, SIBA obligation, fine schedule | [dre.pt](https://dre.pt) search "23/2007" |
| Lei 56/2023 | "Mais Habitação" — most of its AL provisions are reversed by DL 76/2024 | [dre.pt](https://dre.pt) search "56/2023" |
| Regulamento (UE) 2024/1028 | EU short-term rental data sharing regulation, May 2026 | [eur-lex.europa.eu](https://eur-lex.europa.eu) search "2024/1028" |
