# Algarve Alojamento Local (AL) — Frequently Asked Questions

Plain-English answers to the questions British villa owners in the Algarve ask most often when setting up or renewing their AL licence.

---

## 1. Do I need an AL licence to rent my Algarve villa on Airbnb?

Yes. Under **Decreto-Lei 76/2024**, any property let to tourists for less than 30 days at a time must be registered as Alojamento Local through the **Registo Nacional de Alojamento Local (RNAL)** at Turismo de Portugal. Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo all now require the AL number to be displayed on the listing — listings without it are suspended.

See: [algarvepropertycompliance.com](https://algarvepropertycompliance.com/)

---

## 2. How long does it take to get an AL number?

The **comunicação prévia** to your câmara is submitted through the **Balcão Único Eletrónico (BUÉ)** at eportugal.gov.pt. The câmara then has 60 days to oppose (90 days in designated containment areas). If they don't oppose, your AL number is automatically issued.

In practice, the bottleneck is preparing the documents — most British owners take 2–4 weeks to assemble everything (Caderneta Predial, Licença de Utilização, NIF, fiscal rep mandate). The câmara wait is then 60–90 days.

Plan for **3–4 months total** from "I want to rent" to "AL number live".

---

## 3. What happens if I let without an AL number?

Lei 23/2007 + DL 76/2024 fines for unlicensed letting are **€2,500 to €25,000** depending on the volume of bookings. The câmara can also seek a *cessação* order — physically closing the property to tourist use. In practice, the first breach typically draws a €4,500–€8,000 fine plus mandatory regularisation within 30 days.

---

## 4. Do I need a Portuguese fiscal representative?

If you live outside the EU/EEA, **yes**. UK owners post-Brexit fall into this category. The fiscal representative must:

- Be a Portuguese tax resident
- Hold a NIF
- Sign a mandate accepting fiscal representation
- Be registered with Finanças against your NIF

Most British owners use either a lawyer, an accountant, or a specialist fiscal-rep service. Fees range €150–€400 per year.

---

## 5. What is SIBA and do I have to file it?

**SIBA** = *Sistema de Informação de Boletins de Alojamento*. Every guest who is not a Portuguese national must be reported to AIMA (formerly SEF) within **3 working days** of arrival. This includes British, Irish, German, Dutch, French — every nationality.

The portal is **siba.sef.pt**. Login credentials are separate from your Finanças login and take 2–4 weeks to issue. Apply for them before you take your first booking.

See: [https://algarvepropertycompliance.com/news/](https://algarvepropertycompliance.com/news/)

## 6. What CAE code do I register under at Finanças?

For most AL operations: **CAE 55204** (*Outros locais de alojamento de curta duração*). This is the standard short-term rental code introduced in 2024.

- **55201** — Hotels (not relevant for villas)
- **55202** — Tourism apartments (regulated apartment blocks, not standalone villas)
- **55204** — Other short-stay accommodation (this is you)

Use 55204 unless your fiscal representative advises otherwise based on your specific structure.

---

## 7. Do I need a *livro de reclamações*?

Yes. The complaints book is mandatory for every AL. You can choose:

- **Paper version** — buy from Imprensa Nacional or a stationer
- **Electronic version** — register at livroreclamacoes.pt

The complaints book is checked in every ASAE / Câmara inspection. Missing it is an automatic €750–€1,500 fine.

---

## 8. What safety equipment is mandatory?

At minimum:

- **Smoke detectors** in every bedroom and on every floor
- **Fire extinguisher** (powder, 6 kg minimum) in an accessible location
- **Fire blanket** in the kitchen
- **First aid kit** clearly marked
- **Emergency contacts** posted visibly (112, AL operator, manager)

For villas above 2 floors or with capacity > 10, additional regulations apply.

---

## 9. Can I run AL through a Portuguese company instead of personally?

Yes — many British owners structure it through a Portuguese **Sociedade por Quotas** or **Sociedade Unipessoal**. Pros: limited liability, IRC at 21% rather than IRS slabs, easier expense deduction. Cons: setup cost (€500–€1,500), annual accountancy (€100–€200/month), and the company must hold or lease the property.

For 1–2 properties, individual letting under IRS Categoria F (or B if professionalised) is usually simpler. For 3+ properties, company structure typically wins.

---

## 10. What changes in May 2026?

**EU Regulation 2024/1028** comes into force. Every short-term rental platform must:

- Validate your AL number against RNAL at listing creation
- Share booking data monthly with the Portuguese Single Digital Entry Point (SDEP)
- Suspend listings whose AL number is invalid

The SDEP cross-references with Finanças. Mismatches between platform-reported income and your IRS filing will trigger audits within 90 days.

If your AL number, listing name, and IRS filing aren't in sync, fix it before May 2026.
