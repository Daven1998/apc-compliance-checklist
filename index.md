## What is Alojamento Local?

Alojamento Local (AL) is the Portuguese legal regime for short-term tourist rentals — properties let to guests for less than 30 days at a time. If you rent your Algarve villa on Airbnb, Booking.com or Vrbo, you are legally required to register the property in the **Registo Nacional de Alojamento Local (RNAL)** and comply with the obligations set out in **Decreto-Lei 76/2024** and **Lei 23/2007**.

This repository is the consolidated, plain-English version of those obligations — written for British owners who would otherwise have to piece it together from six Portuguese government portals.

---

## The 9 obligations every AL property must satisfy

1. **RNAL registration** at Turismo de Portugal — your AL/2024/xxxxx number
2. **Comunicação prévia** to the câmara via the Balcão Único Eletrónico
3. **Início de Atividade** at Finanças under CAE 55204 (most common AL classification)
4. **NIF + fiscal representative** for non-resident owners (UK owners post-Brexit must have one)
5. **SIBA boletins** for every non-Portuguese guest within 3 working days of arrival
6. **Livro de reclamações** (paper or electronic complaints book)
7. **Safety equipment** — smoke detectors, fire extinguisher, fire blanket, first aid kit
8. **AL identification plaque** on the property exterior
9. **Annual IRS / IRC filing** on rental income (Categoria F or B for individuals, IRC for companies)

## What changed in 2024 — Decreto-Lei 76/2024

The October 2024 reform reversed most of the 2023 "Mais Habitação" crackdown:

- **Restored indefinite AL licences** (no more 5-year expiry)
- **Scrapped the CEAL** (Contribuição Extraordinária do Alojamento Local) retroactively
- **Restored câmara opposition windows** of 60 days (90 in containment areas)
- **Kept all SIBA + Turismo de Portugal obligations** unchanged

If you registered before October 2024 under the old regime, your licence transitioned automatically. If you are registering now, you operate under DL 76/2024.

## What changes in May 2026 — EU Regulation 2024/1028

The EU Short-Term Rental Data Sharing Regulation comes into force on **9 May 2026**. Every short-term rental platform operating in the EU will share host and property data monthly with a Portuguese **Single Digital Entry Point (SDEP)**.

The SDEP cross-references platform data with the RNAL registry and with Finanças. The Algarve — Portugal's largest tourist region by AL count — will be the first place where the gap between licensed properties (~32,000) and listed properties (~41,000) is closed algorithmically.

If you let on Airbnb, Booking.com or Vrbo, make sure before 9 May 2026 that:

1. Your AL number is displayed on every listing
2. The AL number matches the actual property address
3. The host name matches the AL registration *titular*
4. Your IRS filing reconciles with platform-reported income

## Inspections and fines

| Breach | Authority | Typical fine |
|---|---|---|
| Letting without AL registration | Câmara | €2,500 – €25,000 |
| Late or missing SIBA boletin | AIMA | €100 – €4,500 per missed boletin |
| Missing complaints book | ASAE | €750 – €1,500 |
| Missing safety equipment | ASAE / Câmara | €500 – €3,000 |
| AL number mismatch on platform | Câmara + platform suspension | €2,500 – €4,500 + listing pulled |
| Unreported rental income | Finanças | 4-year audit + interest + penalties |
